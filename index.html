App estimate history chart

See how one iOS app's monthly downloads and monthly revenue estimates moved, day by day, over up to a year. Paste an estimate history response below. Nothing leaves your browser.

Where to get the JSON

The input is the estimate history response from PeekPanda, an iOS competitor catalogue that keeps dated evidence and nightly download and revenue estimates for each app. Use the app's numeric App Store id, the number after id in its apps.apple.com link:

curl --get https://api.peekpanda.com/v1/competitors/ios/1234567890/estimates/history \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $PEEKPANDA_TOKEN" \
  -H 'x-genviral-tenancy: personal' \
  --data-urlencode 'limit=90'

Over MCP this is the get_app tool with action estimates. You need a PeekPanda account and an API key; the estimate history reference lists the fields and errors. limit takes 1 to 366 observations.

How to read it

  • The shaded band is the low-to-high estimate range and the line is the mid. They are worldwide monthly App Store estimates in USD, not numbers from Apple or the developer.
  • "Change in window" compares the newest mid with the oldest day that had a range. Month-over-month compares the newest mid with the previous-month value on the same observation.
  • Days under the floor (below 5,000 a month) and days with no estimate are counted, never drawn as zero.
  • Use the ranges for direction and scale, not for exact figures.
